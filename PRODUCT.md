# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Jay uses Usagi as a private desktop AIOS for chat, memory, Obsidian search, tasks, skills, knowledge, and approved actions.

## Product Purpose
Usagi helps Jay work with personal context through a simple local application. Success means the app feels personal and approachable while keeping repeated workflows and approvals easy to operate.

## Brand Personality
Cute utility, soft, helpful. The mascot stays the visual identity, and the dashboard should feel like it belongs around that character.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, dark terminal-heavy shells, sterile admin panels, and visual clutter that makes the tool feel less personal.

## Design Principles
- Put Usagi at the center of the product identity.
- Keep the app practical before decorative.
- Make approvals and AIOS controls easy to scan.
- Use softness and color to support the mascot, not distract from work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Prioritize readable text, clear button states, calm color contrast, and no decorative motion.
